AI EXPO KOREA 2025

AI EXPO KOREA · AI Excellence Summit

CUBIG received the AI Medical Innovation Award at the 2025 AI Excellence Summit. The summit was held during AI EXPO KOREA 2025 in Seoul.

CUBIG described the award as recognition for balancing patient data security with data use in healthcare AI. It said its solution handles several forms of medical data together: images, clinical text, and numerical records.

What CUBIG brought

  1. 01Preparing medical data for AI training while keeping its statistical value
  2. 02Handling several forms of medical data: images, clinical text, numerical records
