AI-Ready Data: What Changes When AI Goes to Production

Alex Wang, GenAI Works · with Ho Bae, Co-founder and CEO of CUBIG

AI-ready data sounds simple until AI actually goes into production. Alex Wang, Head of Education Strategy at GenAI Works, invited Ho Bae to talk through what changes once AI moves beyond experiments.

CUBIG works on the data layer between enterprise systems and the AI models and agents companies want to put to work.

What they talked about

  1. 01Why AI-ready depends on the task, not the dataset
  2. 02Why clean data can still miss the business context AI needs
  3. 03What data work actually lets AI do, and how to check it
  4. 04Build vs. buy, and the work that returns with every change
