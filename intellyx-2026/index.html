2026 Intellyx Digital Innovator Award

Intellyx, industry analyst firm

Intellyx named CUBIG a winner of its 2026 Digital Innovator Award on June 4, 2026. The award goes to vendors that pass the firm’s briefing selection and brief its analysts.

The recognition centers on the operational data layer between an AI pilot and production.

What CUBIG brought

  1. 01The operational data layer between an AI pilot and production
  2. 02A clear view of where enterprise AI stalls: when a working model meets real enterprise data, not at the proof of concept
  3. 03A way to make data that can’t be used as it is, can’t be learned from, or keeps shifting usable for the AI task, checked against it and reproducible
